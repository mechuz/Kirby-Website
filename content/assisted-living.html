Assisted Living

Assisted Living Designed Around Daily Life

Assisted living at The Pointe at Kirby Gate is designed for seniors who want to maintain independence while receiving support with daily activities. Our community provides a balanced approach to care, allowing residents to live confidently while knowing help is always available when needed.

Each resident receives individualized support based on personal needs and preferences. From assistance with daily routines to medication reminders and mobility support, care is delivered with respect, compassion, and consistency.

  • 24-hour professional care team
  • Personalized care plans
  • Medication reminders and mobility support
  • Private and semi-private accommodations
  • Home-style dining, housekeeping, and laundry

Comfort

A Supportive and Comfortable Environment

Residents at The Pointe at Kirby Gate enjoy a comfortable living environment that feels residential rather than clinical. Private and semi-private accommodations provide personal space, while shared common areas encourage social interaction and connection.

Our community is intentionally sized to allow for meaningful relationships between residents and staff. This smaller, more personal setting supports familiarity, trust, and attentive care.

Engagement

Engaging Activities and Social Connection

Daily life at The Pointe at Kirby Gate includes activities designed to promote social engagement, mental stimulation, and physical movement. Residents are encouraged to participate at their own pace, choosing activities that align with their interests and abilities.

Social connection plays an important role in overall well-being, and our activity programming helps residents stay active, engaged, and connected within the community.
