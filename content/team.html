First time here?

  1. Open the email from Airtable that says you were invited.
  2. Click Accept and make a password with your work email.
  3. Come back to this page and press Open My Calls Today.

Bookmark this page: kirbygateliving.com/team. On a phone, use “Add to Home Screen”.

Every day

  1. Press Open My Calls Today and pick your name in Whose calls?
  2. Tap a name. The phone number is at the top. Make the call.
  3. Pick What happened on the call? and What’s next? The next call-back date is set for you.
