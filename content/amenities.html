Dining

Good Food, Served Properly

Meals at The Pointe are prepared from scratch by our kitchen team and served restaurant-style in the dining room — not on a tray. Menus rotate with the seasons, favorites are remembered, and special diets (diabetic, low-sodium, soft, and more) are handled by the kitchen without any fuss.

Between meals, the coffee is always on, and the three o’clock happy hour has become a community tradition.

Community Spaces

Renovated Rooms Built for Company

Our common areas have been refreshed top to bottom: a welcoming lobby, a bright dining room, comfortable lounges for visiting and television, and sunlit sitting areas for reading. Outside, three and a half landscaped acres offer walking paths and shaded benches in a quiet residential setting.

Families are welcome any time — to share a meal, join an activity, or simply sit together on the porch.

Activities & Wellness

Something Worth Doing Every Day

Our activity director plans a full monthly calendar: live music and performers, bingo and card games, chair fitness and stretching, devotionals and Bible study, arts and crafts, movie afternoons, birthday and holiday celebrations, and scheduled outings around Memphis.

Participation is always a resident’s choice. Some join everything; others prefer a quiet morning and a good book. Both are welcome here.
