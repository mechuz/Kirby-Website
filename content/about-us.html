About The Pointe at Kirby Gate

Our Community

Serving East Memphis Since 1999

Sixty residences on 3.5 landscaped acres along Quince Road, including a dedicated memory care neighborhood. Small enough that staff know every resident by name.

Under new ownership since 2026, with suites, common areas and furnishings being renovated top to bottom.

Our Approach

Two Levels of Care, One Standard of Service

Assisted living and memory care under one roof, with licensed care on site 24 hours a day. Residents can get more support without leaving the people who know them, and couples with different needs can stay close.

Consistent staffing, personalized care plans and open communication with families.
