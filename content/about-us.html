About The Pointe at Kirby Gate

Where Gracious Hospitality Meets Genuine Care

Our Community

A Storied Address, Thoughtfully Reimagined

Tucked along a tree-lined stretch of Quince Road, The Pointe at Kirby Gate has been a fixture of East Memphis senior living since 1999. Set on three and a half landscaped acres, the community carries the ease of a private residence — morning light in the front rooms, unhurried conversation at dinner, staff who greet residents by name and know how they take their coffee.

Today, under new ownership, that legacy is being polished to a higher standard. Every renovation underway — from refreshed suites to reimagined gathering spaces — is guided by a simple conviction: the later chapters of life deserve the same grace, comfort, and attention to detail as any fine hotel, and the warmth no hotel can offer. This is home.

Our Philosophy

The Art of Feeling at Home

True hospitality is invisible. It is the fresh linen without asking, the favorite chair kept just so, the caregiver who notices a quiet morning and stops to sit awhile. At The Pointe at Kirby Gate, we practice hospitality the way the great houses do — anticipating needs rather than waiting for them — and we pair it with something rarer: genuine, clinical-grade care delivered by people who consider this work a calling.

Our intentionally intimate size makes that possible. With just sixty residences, no one here is a room number. Preferences are remembered. Routines are honored. Families are welcomed like old friends.

Seamless Care

Two Levels of Care, One Standard of Service

The Pointe offers assisted living and dedicated memory care beneath one roof, so residents can receive precisely the support they need today — and tomorrow — without leaving the community they know or the people who know them. For couples with different care needs, it means staying close. For families planning ahead, it means peace of mind.

Whatever the level of care, the standard does not change: attentive, dignified, personal.

Our Promise

Service That Knows Your Name

Our professional care team is present around the clock — not hovering, simply there, the way good service always is. Days at The Pointe unfold over chef-prepared meals served restaurant-style, engaging programming chosen with residents rather than for them, and quiet corners made for reading, visiting, and afternoon sun. Housekeeping and laundry are simply taken care of, because they should be.

And behind the graciousness stands substance: consistent staffing, personalized care plans, and open, honest communication with families — the details that turn confidence into trust.

The Quiet Side of the City

East Memphis offers a rare balance: minutes from the region’s finest medical providers, shopping, and dining, yet wrapped in the calm of an established residential neighborhood. Families visit easily. Residents keep the city close and the noise far away.

We would be delighted to show you around — the best way to understand The Pointe is simply to walk through the front door.
