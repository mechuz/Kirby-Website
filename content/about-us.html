What We Offer

Care and Services Under One Roof

Families usually come to us with one of three needs. Each has its own page with details, photos and answers to common questions.

  • Assisted living for seniors who want their independence with help close by: medications managed, help with personal care when needed, and a care team on site 24 hours a day. Renovated private and companion suites, chef-prepared meals, housekeeping, linen and laundry.
  • Memory care for people living with Alzheimer’s disease or another dementia: a secured neighborhood, staff trained in dementia care, structured daily routines and music and sensory programming.
  • Respite stays for families who need a break, a trial stay or support after a hospital stay: a furnished suite, meals and care, with a 5-night minimum.

The Pointe at Kirby Gate is private pay. Our current rates are published on the Rates & Floor Plans page, and our guide to paying for senior living explains the options families commonly use.
