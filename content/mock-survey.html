Kirby Gate Team

Live Mock Survey

For Kirby Gate department heads, directors and ownership. Daily department checklists, corrective actions, scheduled audits and the survey binder, live on every iPad.

Sign in to the Mock Survey

Sign in with the Claude account ownership invited you with. If it says you don't have access, ask ownership to share the survey with you.

Set up a department iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari: kirbygateliving.com/mock-survey
  2. Tap Sign in to the Mock Survey and sign in.
  3. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. In the survey, open Daily checklists and, under Set up this iPad, choose your department. The iPad will open straight to that checklist.

Daily rules

  • Submit your checklist by the end of the business day. Findings are reviewed at the 9:00 AM manager meeting.
  • Immediate resident-safety concerns are escalated to the Administrator and DON at once, never held for the morning meeting.
  • Use room numbers or initials in findings, not resident names.
