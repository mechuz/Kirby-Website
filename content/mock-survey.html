Kirby Gate Team

Live Mock Survey

For Kirby Gate department heads, directors and ownership. Daily department checklists, corrective actions, scheduled audits and the survey binder, live on your phone.

Sign in to the Mock Survey

Sign in with the same email ownership invited. Don't have a Claude account yet? Create one with that email first. If it says you don't have access, ask ownership to share the survey with you.

Put it on your phone's home screen

  1. On your phone, open kirbygateliving.com/mock-survey and tap Sign in to the Mock Survey.
  2. Sign in once. Your phone remembers you.
  3. iPhone: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen.
  4. In the survey, open Checklist and, under Set up this phone, choose your department. The app will then open straight to your checklist.

Daily rules

  • Department heads finish and submit their checklist by 2:30 PM every day. Leadership gets the day's results at 3:00 PM, and open findings are reviewed at the 9:00 AM manager meeting.
  • Immediate resident-safety concerns are escalated to the Administrator and DON at once, never held for the morning meeting.
  • Use room numbers or initials in findings, not resident names.
