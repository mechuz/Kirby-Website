Holiday gatherings are often when siblings and other relatives first see how much an older parent has changed. AARP's caregiving guide suggests keeping these conversations calm and specific: skip blaming statements, ask relatives what they have noticed about the parent's walking, speech or memory, and turn offers of help into concrete tasks someone can take on. It also encourages caregivers to let others share the load rather than carrying it alone through the season.

Why it matters: Thanksgiving is a natural time for families to notice a parent needs more support and to start planning together, before a crisis forces the decision.
