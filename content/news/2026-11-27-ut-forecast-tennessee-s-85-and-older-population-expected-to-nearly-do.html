The University of Tennessee's Boyd Center for Business and Economic Research has updated its population forecast and now projects that Tennessee will reach about 8.1 million residents by 2040, roughly two years sooner than its previous estimate. The fastest growth is expected among the state's oldest residents. Tennesseans 65 and older are projected to increase from about 1.32 million in 2024 to about 1.68 million in 2040, and the 85-and-older group is expected to grow by about 94.5%, nearly doubling. The Boyd Center also publishes county-level projections, including for Shelby County, through the Tennessee State Data Center.

Why it matters: A fast-growing number of Tennesseans in their 80s and 90s means more Mid-South families will be planning for care in the years ahead.
