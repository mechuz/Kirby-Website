Senior Housing News covers a new industry report that looks at why demand for senior living is strong while construction of new communities is not. One finding concerns staffing: it took an average of 48% of revenue in 2025, compared with 39% ten years earlier. The authors say the main driver is probably that residents now arrive needing more care than in the past, more than the extra staff required as buildings fill up.

Why it matters: Few new communities and higher care needs can mean fewer openings, which is a reason for families to start looking early.
