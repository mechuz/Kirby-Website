We have opened Family Resources, a library of plain-language guides for families comparing assisted living and memory care in East Memphis, written by the people who answer these questions every day. The first guide, Questions to Ask on an Assisted Living Tour, covers what to ask about care, staffing, daily life, cost and contracts, and what to notice while you are in the building. More guides are on the way.

Why it matters: a tour is the one part of this decision you can do in person, and the right questions make it count.
