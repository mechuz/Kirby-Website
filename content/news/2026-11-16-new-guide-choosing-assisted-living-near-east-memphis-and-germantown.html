Our new guide is for families comparing assisted living close to home in East Memphis, Germantown and Collierville. It suggests starting with the relative who will visit most, driving the route at a real visiting hour, and keeping a parent's doctors, pharmacy and weekly routines within reach. It explains how Tennessee licenses assisted care living facilities, how to ask a community for its latest state survey, and how the Long-Term Care Ombudsman can help. It also covers what to look for when a parent has memory loss and ends with a checklist for comparing nearby communities.

Why it matters: Distance narrows the list, but level of care, inspection history and a parent's routines decide the right fit.
