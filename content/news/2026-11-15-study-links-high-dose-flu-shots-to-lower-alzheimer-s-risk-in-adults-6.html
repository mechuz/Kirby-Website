Researchers at UTHealth Houston, publishing in the journal Neurology, compared older adults who received a high-dose flu vaccine with those who received the standard dose. In this large records-based study, the high-dose group was less likely to be diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease in the years that followed, and the link appeared stronger in women. Because the study was observational, it shows an association, not proof that the vaccine prevents Alzheimer's, and the authors call for further research.

Why it matters: Flu season is here; families with an older parent can ask their doctor which flu vaccine is right for them.
