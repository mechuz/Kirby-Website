Medicare's yearly open enrollment runs from October 15 through December 7, 2026, and any change made in that window takes effect on January 1. Among the shifts arriving in 2027 are a higher yearly cap on out-of-pocket drug costs in Part D, the end of a temporary program that had held down some standalone drug plan premiums, and new negotiated prices for 15 more high-cost medicines. Some Medicare Advantage plans are also leaving certain areas or trimming benefits, so the plan's annual notice of change is worth reading closely.

Why it matters: If a parent's plan or medicines are changing, a free Medicare counselor (SHIP) can help compare options before December 7.
