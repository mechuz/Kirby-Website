With the holidays coming, our new guide offers simple ways to keep visits calm and meaningful for a parent living with dementia. It covers planning shorter, quieter visits, adapting favorite traditions, choosing gifts that suit the person today, and preparing relatives who have not visited in a while. It also reminds family caregivers to plan support for themselves during a busy season.

Why it matters: Small changes to holiday plans can make the season easier for a parent with dementia and for the whole family.
