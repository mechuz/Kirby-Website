The Alzheimer's Association's 2026 Facts and Figures report estimates that 7.4 million Americans age 65 and older are living with Alzheimer's, and that more than 12 million family members and friends provided unpaid care last year, most of them describing the emotional strain as high. A companion survey found that nearly everyone values brain health, yet fewer than one in ten feel they know much about protecting it.

Why it matters: the report lists everyday habits linked to lower risk, such as staying active, sleeping well and managing blood pressure, which makes a good conversation to have with a parent's doctor.
