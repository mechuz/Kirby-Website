The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services has finalized its 2027 payment rule for Medicare home health agencies, the providers that send nurses and therapists to the homes of eligible, homebound beneficiaries. According to Home Health Care News, the rule raises payments by 2.4%, the same increase as in 2026 and about $415 million more overall. It also adds several fraud-prevention measures, most of which apply to every type of Medicare-enrolled provider, and recognizes home health's role in palliative care, with examples to follow in later guidance. CMS did not finalize a new home health wage index method. The provisions take effect January 1, 2027.

Why it matters: Medicare home health is often part of a hospital discharge plan for an older adult, and this rule shapes how those agencies are paid and screened starting in January.
