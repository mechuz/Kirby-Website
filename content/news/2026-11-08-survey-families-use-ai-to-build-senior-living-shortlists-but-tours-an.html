A U.S. News & World Report survey of 502 U.S. adults who were researching senior living, or had recently moved a relative or themselves into a community, found that about 70% used generative AI tools during the search, most often to compare communities on a shortlist. About half of those able to judge said they had run into wrong AI-generated information, most often about the level of care or amenities a community offers and about availability or wait times. Final decisions still hinged on in-person tours and conversations with staff and residents, and three in four respondents said they would check an independent source before choosing.

Why it matters: AI tools can speed up a search, but care levels and availability are worth confirming directly with each community, in person.
