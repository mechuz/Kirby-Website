An Alzheimer's researcher at the University of Kansas told Senior Housing News that the system for diagnosing dementia has fallen behind the newer treatments now available. He pointed to memory clinic waitlists that can run for months and a shortage of dementia specialists as the main barriers. The article notes that 44% of assisted living residents live with Alzheimer's or another form of dementia, which makes earlier and faster diagnosis a growing concern for families and the communities that care for them.

Why it matters: An earlier diagnosis gives a family more time to plan care and choose the right setting before a crisis.
