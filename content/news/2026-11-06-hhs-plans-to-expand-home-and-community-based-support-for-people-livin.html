Home Health Care News reports that the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services plans to improve access to home- and community-based services for people living with dementia as part of its federal dementia-care priorities. Those priorities also include stronger support for family caregivers, including respite and education, and better coordination of care. The article points to the federal GUIDE model, which already takes a home-based approach to dementia care, as an example of this direction.

Why it matters: Families caring for a loved one with dementia at home may see more local support, including respite and caregiver education, as these federal priorities take shape.
