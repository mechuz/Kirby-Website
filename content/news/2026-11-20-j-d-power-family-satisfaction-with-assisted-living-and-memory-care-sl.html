Power's 2026 U.S. Senior Living Satisfaction Study found that family members and other decision-makers for assisted living and memory care residents were somewhat less satisfied than a year earlier. The segment's overall score fell four points to 863 on a 1,000-point scale. Four of the six areas measured declined: community staff, resident activities, the apartment or living unit, and buildings and grounds. Independent living moved the other way, with resident satisfaction up 14 points to 767. The assisted living and memory care results come from 860 responses collected from June through August 2026.

Why it matters: The study tracks how families rate the parts of assisted living and memory care they see most, including staff, daily activities and living spaces.
