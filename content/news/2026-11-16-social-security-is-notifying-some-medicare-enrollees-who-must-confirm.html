The Social Security Administration says a federal law enacted in July 2025 narrows who can be entitled to Medicare. Eligibility is now limited to U.S. citizens and nationals, lawful permanent residents and a few other specific immigration categories. SSA is mailing letters this month to current enrollees whose eligibility it cannot confirm or who no longer qualify, and it says Medicare coverage may end on February 1, 2027 for people whose eligibility is not confirmed. The agency notes the change does not affect eligibility for Social Security benefits or how they are paid. CMS actuaries estimate about 32,000 people will lose coverage.

Why it matters: Families who help an older relative with mail should watch for these letters, since SSA says unconfirmed eligibility can end Medicare coverage on February 1.
