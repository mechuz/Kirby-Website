Senior Housing News looked at how the senior living market is shaping up in the second half of 2026. Citing NIC MAP data, it reports that average occupancy across the 31 largest U.S. markets reached 90.4% in the third quarter, with assisted living rising to about 89.1%. The gains are uneven: assisted living and independent living each absorbed roughly 3% to 3.6% of existing inventory this year, while properties focused mainly on memory care, along with continuing care retirement communities, saw almost no net growth. Construction pipelines have shrunk by roughly 60% over five years, and investors are generally buying existing communities rather than building new ones.

Why it matters: With little new construction and occupancy rising, families in many markets may find fewer immediate openings than in past years.
