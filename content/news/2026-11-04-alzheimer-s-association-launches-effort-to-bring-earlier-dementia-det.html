The Alzheimer's Association has launched a national Brain Health and Quality Care Initiative to help health systems build brain health, early detection, diagnosis and dementia care into routine medical care, starting in primary care practices. The goal is for more people to have memory concerns checked and diagnosed earlier, closer to home, rather than waiting for a specialist. The effort builds on the Association's guidance for health systems on running dementia care programs.

Why it matters: If a parent's memory is changing, the family doctor is a good first stop; earlier diagnosis gives a family more time to plan.
