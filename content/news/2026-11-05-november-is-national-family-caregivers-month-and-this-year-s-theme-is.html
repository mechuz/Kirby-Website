Caregiver Action Network, which has led National Family Caregivers Month every November since 1997, chose "Care Connects Us" as the 2026 theme. The month-long campaign is aimed at the millions of Americans caring for a parent, spouse or other loved one, with the goal of helping them feel less alone and connecting them with information and support. The organization's site lists resources for caregivers throughout the month. Locally, the Aging Commission of the Mid-South runs a Family Caregiver Program for Shelby County families.

Why it matters: A good moment to take stock of the help available to you as a caregiver; in Shelby County, the Aging Commission of the Mid-South's information line is 901-222-4111.
