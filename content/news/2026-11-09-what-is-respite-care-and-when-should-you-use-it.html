Our new guide explains respite care: short-term care for an older adult so the usual family caregiver can rest, travel or recover. It describes the main forms, from in-home help and adult day programs to short residential stays, and lists warning signs that a caregiver needs a break, such as constant exhaustion and putting off their own health care. It also covers what to look for when a parent has dementia, where Memphis families can find caregiver support, and a checklist for planning a stay that goes smoothly.

Why it matters: Planning a break before burnout sets in helps family caregivers keep caring for a parent over the long run.
