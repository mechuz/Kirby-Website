The National Council on Aging has updated its flu season guide for 2026-2027. NCOA reports that in a September 1 update the CDC kept its earlier seasonal flu vaccine recommendations in place, including a preference for certain enhanced vaccines for people 65 and older. The guide notes that in August 2026 the FDA approved the first mRNA flu vaccine in the U.S. for adults 50 and older. NCOA adds that a flu shot can still be given later in the season, that the vaccine lowers the risk of severe illness even if it does not prevent every case, and when to seek care after getting sick.

Why it matters: With flu season starting and family gatherings ahead, it is a good time to talk to your doctor, or a parent's doctor, about which vaccines fit.
