CMS announced negotiated prices for 13 more prescription drugs in the third round of Medicare's drug price negotiation program. The new prices take effect January 1, 2028, bringing the number of drugs with negotiated Medicare prices to 37. This round is the first to include Part B drugs, which are given in doctors' offices and clinics, alongside Part D pharmacy drugs, and the first to renegotiate a drug from an earlier round. CMS estimates Medicare's net spending on these drugs would have been about 45%, or $9.2 billion, lower if the prices had applied in 2025. The negotiated prices are what Medicare pays, not necessarily what enrollees pay at the counter.

Why it matters: CMS projects some out-of-pocket savings for enrollees once the prices apply; a benefits counselor can explain how a specific plan will be affected.
