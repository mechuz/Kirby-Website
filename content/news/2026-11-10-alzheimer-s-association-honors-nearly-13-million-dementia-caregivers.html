For National Family Caregivers Month, the Alzheimer's Association is recognizing the nearly 13 million Americans who give unpaid care to someone living with Alzheimer's or another dementia, under the 2026 theme "Caregiving Changes Everything." The Association notes that a diagnosis reshapes daily life for the whole family, and it is sharing stories from spouses, adult children, grandchildren and friends throughout November. It also points caregivers to its free resources, including a 24/7 Helpline, support groups, care planning tools, education programs and a monthly on-demand webinar series. Anyone can send a note of thanks to a caregiver through the Association's Honor a Caregiver campaign.

Why it matters: Caring for someone with dementia is a long road, and free, around-the-clock support is available so no family has to manage it alone.
