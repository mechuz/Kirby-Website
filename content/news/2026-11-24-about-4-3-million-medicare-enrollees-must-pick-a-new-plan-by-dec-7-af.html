NPR reports that an estimated 4.3 million people will need to choose a new Medicare Advantage or prescription drug plan for 2027 because insurers are pulling out of certain markets. Open enrollment ends December 7, and coverage chosen now begins January 1. People whose Advantage plan is ending and who take no action are moved to traditional Medicare, which does not include drug coverage by itself, so they would need a separate Part D plan to keep most prescriptions covered. The story also walks through other 2027 changes and encourages enrollees to read their plan notices and compare options before the deadline.

Why it matters: If a parent got a notice that their plan is ending, there are only days left to compare options; a benefits counselor can help.
