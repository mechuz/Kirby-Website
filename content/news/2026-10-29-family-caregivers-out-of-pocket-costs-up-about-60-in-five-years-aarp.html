New AARP research reported by NPR finds that family caregivers now spend an average of about $11,508 a year of their own money on caregiving, roughly 60% more than five years ago. That amounts to around 31% of a typical caregiver's income, and the share is far higher for lower-income households. The story frames the cost of caregiving as rising even faster than rent or gas, putting pressure on family budgets and retirement savings.

Why it matters: Caring for a parent at home has real costs that are easy to underestimate; a benefits counselor can help a family look at the full picture.
