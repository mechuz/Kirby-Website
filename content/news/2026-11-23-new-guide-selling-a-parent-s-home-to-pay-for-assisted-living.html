Our new guide is for families weighing whether to sell a parent's house when they move to assisted living or memory care. It starts with the family conversation: involving your parent, confirming who has legal authority to sign, and keeping siblings informed. It then explains in plain language how the IRS home sale exclusion works, including a special rule for people who can no longer care for themselves, with links to the official IRS publications. It is general information, and families are encouraged to confirm details with a tax professional or attorney.

Why it matters: A clear starting point for the questions families ask most before listing a parent's home.
