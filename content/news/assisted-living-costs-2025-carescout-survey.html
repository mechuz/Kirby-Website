CareScout's annual Cost of Care Survey, drawn from roughly 16,000 responses from long-term care providers across the country, found that the national median cost of an assisted living community rose about 5 percent in 2025. Home care and nursing home rates rose as well, and a private nursing home room still runs roughly three-quarters more than assisted living. The report breaks the medians down by state and by type of care.

Why it matters: rates vary a great deal from one community to the next, so ask exactly what a monthly rate includes before you compare.
