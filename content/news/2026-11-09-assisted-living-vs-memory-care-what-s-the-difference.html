Our new guide compares assisted living and memory care, two settings that are easy to confuse. It explains how they differ in who they serve, safety and secured spaces, dementia training for staff, and how daily life is structured. It notes that in Tennessee, assisted living is licensed by the state's Health Facilities Commission and is not a nursing home. The guide also lists signs that a parent with memory loss may need memory care, and questions worth bringing on a tour.

Why it matters: Choosing the right setting from the start can spare a parent a second move later.
