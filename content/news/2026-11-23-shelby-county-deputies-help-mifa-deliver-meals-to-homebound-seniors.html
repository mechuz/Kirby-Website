Staff from the Shelby County Sheriff's Office joined the Metropolitan Inter-Faith Association (MIFA) last week to help bring meals to older adults in Memphis. WREG reports that more than 30 sheriff's office employees delivered boxes of shelf-stable food, with five meals in each box. MIFA's Meals on Wheels program serves homebound seniors age 60 and older with daily meal deliveries, and seniors are referred to the program through the Aging Commission of the Mid-South.

Why it matters: If you worry about a parent eating well at home, the Aging Commission of the Mid-South can tell you how meal delivery referrals work.
