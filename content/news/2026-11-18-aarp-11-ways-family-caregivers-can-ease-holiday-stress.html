AARP's guide for family caregivers suggests ways to make the holiday season more manageable. Its ideas include bringing in extra help, such as respite care or a hired helper for chores and decorating, and sharing the cooking by making meals potluck-style. It also suggests starting new traditions that fit what a loved one can comfortably do now, and keeping gatherings shorter with time set aside for rest.

Why it matters: With Thanksgiving next week, small changes to the plan can make the day easier for the caregiver and for the person receiving care.
