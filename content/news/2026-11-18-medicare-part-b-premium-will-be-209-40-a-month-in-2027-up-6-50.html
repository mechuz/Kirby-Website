The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services has set the standard Medicare Part B premium for 2027 at $209.40 a month, an increase of $6.50 from $202.90 this year. The yearly Part B deductible will rise by $9, from $283 to $292. The agency says the increases mainly reflect projected growth in health care spending. People with higher incomes pay more than the standard amount. According to the Social Security Administration, enrollees can see their own 2027 premium in the message center of their my Social Security account starting in late November.

Why it matters: Part B is usually taken out of the Social Security payment, so the new premium changes the monthly income a family has to plan with.
