A group of national senior living organizations, including LeadingAge, Argentum, the American Seniors Housing Association, the National Center for Assisted Living and the National Association for Regulatory Administration, has released a set of best-practice guidelines for how assisted living communities plan for emergencies such as fires, severe weather and outbreaks. The guidelines follow a public comment period on a 2025 draft and come with a readiness scorecard communities can use to check their own planning. The groups describe them as voluntary guidance rather than new rules; each state still sets its own requirements.

Why it matters: When you tour, it is fair to ask how a community plans for storms, power outages and emergencies, and who on staff is responsible for that plan.
