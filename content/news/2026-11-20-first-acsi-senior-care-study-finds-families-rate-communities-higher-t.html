The American Customer Satisfaction Index released its first Senior Care Communities Study, based on 2,830 completed surveys. Family members scored their satisfaction at 73 on a 100-point scale, and residents scored theirs at 62. Families scored higher across the study's customer experience measures, and the gap was widest on trust, at 75 for families and 59 for residents. Assisted living had the highest satisfaction of the settings studied. The largest differences between residents and families were in skilled nursing and independent living. More than a third of residents and family members said they had filed a complaint.

Why it matters: A resident's own view of daily life can differ a lot from the family's, which matters when comparing communities or checking in after a move.
