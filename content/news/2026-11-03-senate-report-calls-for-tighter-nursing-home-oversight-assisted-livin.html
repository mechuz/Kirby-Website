A new report from Democrats on the Senate Finance Committee calls for closer federal oversight of nursing homes, including more transparency about who owns them and stricter staffing standards. Senior living groups are watching because the push could spread to other parts of long-term care. The assisted living trade group Argentum noted that the report does not recommend new federal requirements for assisted living, which continues to be licensed and inspected by each state.

Why it matters: Assisted living rules come from the state, so families comparing communities can ask to see the most recent state inspection report.
