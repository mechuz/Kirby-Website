Senior Housing News reports on a new forecast from real estate research firm Green Street, which lowered its outlook for new senior living supply and now expects growth of roughly 1% in 2029 and 3% in 2030. Most of the projects moving ahead are aimed at the high end of the market, while elevated construction and borrowing costs keep many middle-market projects from getting built. Developers also generally need an experienced operator in place to secure financing.

Why it matters: With little new construction coming, openings in established communities may stay limited, so families benefit from starting their search before a crisis.
