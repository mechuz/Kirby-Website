Our new guide answers a question many families ask during Medicare open enrollment. Medicare does not pay for assisted living, because room, meals and daily personal care are not covered. The guide explains what Medicare still covers when a parent lives in assisted living: doctors, hospital care and prescriptions, part-time home health services, hospice, and short-term skilled nursing rehab after a qualifying hospital stay of at least three days. It also lists questions to ask before the December 7 enrollment deadline and points families to Tennessee's free, unbiased SHIP Medicare counseling.

Why it matters: Knowing what Medicare does and does not cover helps families plan before a hospital discharge forces a decision.
