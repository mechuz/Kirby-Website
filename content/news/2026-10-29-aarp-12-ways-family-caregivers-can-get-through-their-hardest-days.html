AARP collects a dozen practical strategies for family caregivers on days when patience runs thin. The guide covers handling anger when a loved one falls, wanders or makes a mess, including letting frustration out away from the person rather than in front of them. It also addresses dealing with paperwork and bureaucracy, setting boundaries, and making time for interests that caregivers often give up. The advice draws on caregiving researchers, including a Stanford expert on family caregiver stress.

Why it matters: Hard days are a normal part of caring for a parent, and having a few strategies ready can make them easier to get through.
