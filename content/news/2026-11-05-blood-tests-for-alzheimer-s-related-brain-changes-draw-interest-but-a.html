NPR reports that more patients are asking doctors about blood tests that look for changes in the brain linked to Alzheimer's disease. In September, the Food and Drug Administration approved two tests that measure a protein called p-tau217. The experts NPR spoke with stress an important limit: the approved tests are meant for people who already have memory or thinking symptoms, as one part of a doctor's evaluation. They have not been approved for screening people who have no symptoms.

Why it matters: If a parent is showing memory changes, a blood test may come up with their doctor; it is not a screening test for everyone, so talk to your doctor about whether it fits.
