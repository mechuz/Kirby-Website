Our new guide walks families through ten common signs that a parent living with dementia may need more support than home can safely give, such as wandering, missed medicines, falls, changes in eating and a caregiver who is running on empty. It explains what each sign can look like day to day and suggests gentle next steps, from talking with a doctor to touring memory care communities in the Memphis area.

Why it matters: A practical checklist for families who are unsure whether it is time to look at memory care.
