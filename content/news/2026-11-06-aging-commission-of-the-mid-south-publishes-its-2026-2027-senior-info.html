The Aging Commission of the Mid-South has posted the 2026-2027 edition of its Senior Information Handbook on the Shelby County government website. The free guide gathers local resources for older adults and their families in one place, from emergency services to tax and trustee information and other benefits for seniors. It also lists the commission's Information and Assistance line and its downtown Memphis office, and a Spanish-language version is listed on the commission's resources page.

Why it matters: A single local reference for families sorting out services, benefits and contacts for an aging parent in Shelby County.
