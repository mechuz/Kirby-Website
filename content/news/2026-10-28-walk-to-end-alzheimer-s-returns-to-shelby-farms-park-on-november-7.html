The Alzheimer's Association's Memphis Walk to End Alzheimer's takes place on Saturday, November 7, 2026, at Shelby Farms Park. On-site registration opens at 8 a.m. and the Promise Garden ceremony, where walkers carry flowers that stand for their connection to the disease, begins at 9 a.m. Individuals and teams from across the Mid-South take part, and the money raised supports care, support programs and research.

Why it matters: It is a welcoming way for families touched by dementia to meet others in the same place.
