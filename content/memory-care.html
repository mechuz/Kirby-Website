Memory Care

Specialized Memory Care Services

The Pointe at Kirby Gate provides dedicated memory care for residents living with Alzheimer’s disease or other forms of dementia. Our memory care program focuses on safety, familiarity, and individualized support to help residents feel secure and supported each day.

Care plans are thoughtfully developed to reflect each resident’s cognitive needs, personal history, and daily routines.

  • Secure, dedicated memory care neighborhood
  • Specially trained caregivers
  • Consistent staffing and daily routines
  • Cognitive-focused activities and sensory engagement
  • Regular family communication

Safety First

A Secure and Supportive Environment

Memory care at The Pointe at Kirby Gate takes place in a secure area designed to reduce confusion and promote comfort. Clear layouts, consistent routines, and attentive supervision help residents navigate their day with confidence.

This structured environment allows residents to maintain a sense of independence while ensuring their safety and well-being.
