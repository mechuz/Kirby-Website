Common Questions

Memory Care, Answered

What is memory care (dementia care)?

Senior living for people with Alzheimer’s disease or other dementias: a secure setting, staff trained in dementia care, and structured daily activities.

How is memory care different from assisted living?

It adds a secured neighborhood, more staff attention, dementia training and cognitive activities. At The Pointe, both are under one roof, so a move between them stays close to home.

When is it time for memory care?

Common signs are wandering, sundowning, safety scares, caregiver exhaustion and growing confusion with daily tasks. Call (901) 753-3449 to talk it through.

Can I visit my loved one anytime?

Yes. Visits, meals together and joining activities are always welcome.
