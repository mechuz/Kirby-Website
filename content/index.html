Welcome to The Pointe at Kirby Gate

The Pointe at Kirby Gate is an established assisted living and memory care community located in a quiet residential neighborhood in East Memphis. Set on 3.5 acres, the community offers a supportive, comfortable environment designed to help seniors live with dignity, confidence, and peace of mind.

Under new ownership, The Pointe at Kirby Gate is undergoing thoughtful renovations to refresh both its physical spaces and its overall brand presence. These updates reflect a renewed commitment to quality care, safety, and a welcoming atmosphere for residents and families alike. With 60 total beds, including dedicated memory care accommodations, The Pointe at Kirby Gate provides personalized support in a setting that feels calm, familiar, and secure.

Our Services

Two Levels of Care, One Community

Assisted Living with Personalized Support

Designed for seniors who value independence but benefit from daily support. Our professional care team is available 24 hours a day, with private or semi-private accommodations, home-style dining, and daily activities that promote social connection and well-being.

Learn About Assisted Living

Dedicated Memory Care in a Secure Environment

Specialized care for residents living with Alzheimer’s disease or other forms of dementia. Daily routines, cognitive-focused activities, and consistent staffing create familiarity and comfort in a secure, structured neighborhood.

Daily Life

A Comfortable Community Designed for Daily Life

Life at The Pointe at Kirby Gate extends beyond care services. Residents enjoy chef-prepared meals, housekeeping and laundry services, and shared community spaces that encourage connection and relaxation. Daily activities and wellness programming support both physical health and emotional well-being.

Ongoing renovations are enhancing common areas and living spaces to ensure the community continues to meet the needs of today’s seniors while maintaining a warm, residential feel.

Location

Senior Living in East Memphis

The Pointe at Kirby Gate’s location on Quince Road places residents close to local medical providers, shopping, dining, and family access while still offering the peace and quiet of a residential setting. This balance allows residents to enjoy convenience without the noise and congestion of busier areas.

The community’s setting, combined with attentive care and a focus on comfort, makes The Pointe at Kirby Gate a trusted option for assisted living and memory care in Memphis.
