Amenities & Daily Life

The comforts of a fine hotel. The warmth of home. The care of family.

The Pointe Standard

Everything You Need. Nothing You Don’t.

Luxury, at its best, is not excess — it is ease. Every amenity at The Pointe at Kirby Gate exists to make each day simpler, safer, and more enjoyable: meals worth lingering over, spaces that invite company, and a house that quietly runs itself so residents can spend their energy on living.

Dining & Services

The Table Is the Heart of the House

Three chef-prepared meals a day, served restaurant-style in a proper dining room — with favorite dishes remembered and dietary needs handled without fuss. Between meals, coffee is always on, and the three o’clock happy hour is a Pointe institution.

Housekeeping, linens, and laundry are all quietly taken care of, every week, without asking.

  • Chef-prepared breakfast, lunch & dinner daily
  • Restaurant-style service and flexible dining
  • Weekly housekeeping, linen & laundry service
  • 24-hour professional care team on site
  • Medication management available

Spaces & Programming

Rooms Made for Company, Days Made for Living

Freshly renovated lounges, sunlit sitting areas, landscaped grounds with walking paths — The Pointe’s three and a half acres are designed to draw people out of their suites and into good company. The activity calendar does the rest: live music, games, gentle fitness, devotionals, crafts, and celebrations for every occasion worth celebrating.

Every program is an invitation. Residents choose their pace, their people, and their pastimes.
