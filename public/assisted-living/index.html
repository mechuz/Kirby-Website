Common Questions

Assisted Living, Answered

What is assisted living?

A private residence with help for daily activities such as bathing, dressing, medications and mobility, plus meals, housekeeping and activities, with staff available around the clock.

How much does assisted living cost in Memphis?

It depends on the suite and level of care. The Pointe is private pay, and our current rates are on the Rates & Floor Plans page. Call (901) 753-3449 for availability.

How do I know if it’s time for assisted living?

Common signs are missed medications, trouble with meals or housekeeping, growing isolation, or a recent fall.

Can couples live together?

Yes. With assisted living and memory care under one roof, couples with different care needs can stay close.
