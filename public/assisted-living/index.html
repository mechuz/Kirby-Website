Assisted Living at The Pointe

Live on Your Terms, With Help Always Within Reach

Assisted living at The Pointe at Kirby Gate is for seniors who want to keep their independence but benefit from daily support — a steadying hand, medications managed, and help with personal care when needed. Residents set their own routines, and our care team is on site 24 hours a day.

Each resident’s care plan is built around their needs, habits, and preferences, then delivered with consistency by caregivers they know and trust.

  • Daily social, wellness, and enrichment programming

Common Questions

Assisted Living, Answered

What is assisted living?

Assisted living offers seniors private residences with personalized help for daily activities — bathing, dressing, medications, mobility — plus dining, housekeeping, and social programming, all with staff available around the clock. It bridges the gap between living alone and needing full nursing care.

How much does assisted living cost in Memphis?

Pricing depends on suite type and level of care. The Pointe at Kirby Gate is known for offering exceptional value in East Memphis — contact us at (901) 753-3449 for current rates and availability, and we’ll prepare a personalized quote after a brief conversation about your loved one’s needs. We accept private pay, long-term care insurance, VA benefits, and Medicaid (TennCare).

How do I know if it’s time for assisted living?

Common signs include missed medications, difficulty with meals or housekeeping, increasing isolation, or a recent fall. The surest way to know is to talk it through — our team offers honest, no-pressure guidance, even if the answer is “not yet.”

Can couples live together?

Yes. And because we offer both assisted living and memory care under one roof, couples with different care needs can remain close to one another in the same community.
