Common Questions

Assisted Living, Answered

What is assisted living?

Assisted living offers seniors private residences with personalized help for daily activities — bathing, dressing, medications, mobility — plus dining, housekeeping, and social programming, all with staff available around the clock. It bridges the gap between living alone and needing full nursing care.

How much does assisted living cost in Memphis?

Pricing depends on suite type and level of care. The Pointe at Kirby Gate is known for offering exceptional value in East Memphis — contact us at (901) 753-3449 for current rates and availability, and we’ll prepare a personalized quote after a brief conversation about your loved one’s needs.

How do I know if it’s time for assisted living?

Common signs include missed medications, difficulty with meals or housekeeping, increasing isolation, or a recent fall. The surest way to know is to talk it through — our team offers honest, no-pressure guidance, even if the answer is “not yet.”

Can couples live together?

Yes. And because we offer both assisted living and memory care under one roof, couples with different care needs can remain close to one another in the same community.
