Why People Love Working Here

The Pointe at Kirby Gate is a 60-residence assisted living and memory care community in East Memphis, under energetic new ownership and growing. We are small enough that every team member is known by name, and serious enough about care that your work is respected, supported, and noticed.

  • Meaningful WorkYou will know every resident by name — and they will know yours
  • A Real TeamSupportive leadership, consistent scheduling, and coworkers who show up
  • A Community on the RiseNew ownership, fresh renovations, and room to grow with us
  • Flexible ShiftsDay, evening, and overnight schedules across care and support roles
