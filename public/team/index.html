Equal Housing Opportunity Accessible Community

The Pointe at Kirby Gate is committed to the letter and spirit of the federal Fair Housing Act and the Tennessee Human Rights Act. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability, age, or any other characteristic protected by law in the admission, treatment, or services provided to residents.

The Pointe at Kirby Gate is a licensed Assisted Care Living Facility regulated by the Tennessee Department of Health, Board for Licensing Health Care Facilities. Before anyone is approved for residency or a respite stay, they must complete a health assessment with our nursing team; services are then provided under a signed resident agreement; availability, rates, and care levels are subject to change. Information on this website is for general purposes only and is not medical, legal, or financial advice.
