Welcome to The Pointe at Kirby Gate

Senior Living, Elevated

Some places care for you. Fewer places make you feel truly at home. On three and a half landscaped acres in a quiet East Memphis neighborhood, The Pointe at Kirby Gate does both — pairing the warmth and polish of a boutique hotel with attentive, personalized care available twenty-four hours a day.

With just sixty residences, including a dedicated memory care neighborhood, ours is a community where staff know every resident by name, preference, and story. Newly under passionate ownership and in the midst of a thoughtful top-to-bottom renovation, The Pointe is quickly becoming East Memphis’s most inviting address for assisted living and memory care.

Our Services

Two Levels of Care. One Standard of Excellence.

Assisted Living in Memphis

Independence, with a safety net woven in. Private and companion suites, chef-prepared dining, engaging daily programming, and a professional care team on hand around the clock — so residents live on their own terms, and families sleep easy.

Memory Care in Memphis

Life at The Pointe

Days Worth Looking Forward To

Mornings begin with a proper breakfast, not a tray. Afternoons fill with live music, games that draw a crowd, visits from grandchildren, and quiet corners with good light for reading. Evenings end with chef-prepared meals served restaurant-style among neighbors who have become friends.

Housekeeping, laundry, and life’s logistics are quietly handled behind the scenes — because a well-run house should feel effortless to the people who live in it.
