Assisted Living Care Levels

Your Suite Rate + One Care Level = Your Whole Bill

After a nurse assessment, each assisted living resident is matched to one of three care levels. Add it to your suite rate and that’s your total monthly cost — meals, housekeeping, activities, and 24-hour staffing already included.

Level 1
+ $525/mo

A light touch — reminders, stand-by help, and a watchful eye.

Level 2
+ $990/mo

Hands-on help with daily routines like bathing and dressing.
