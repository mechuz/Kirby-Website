Assisted Living Rates

Straight Answers on Pricing

Assisted living rates at The Pointe are based on two things: the suite you choose and the level of care you need. No mystery bundles, no surprise add-ons — just a clear monthly rate that covers your residence, chef-prepared dining, housekeeping, programming, and around-the-clock care availability.

Because care needs are personal, so is pricing. A short conversation lets us quote you accurately the first time.

Memory Care Rates

Specialized Care, Honestly Priced

Memory care rates reflect what sets it apart: a secured neighborhood, enhanced staffing, and caregivers trained specifically in dementia care. Families consistently find The Pointe delivers this level of care at a value few East Memphis communities can match.
