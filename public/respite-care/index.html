Respite Care in Memphis

Short-term stays with full care — for recovery, for caregiver relief, or to try the community before deciding.

Short-Term Stays

Everything a Full-Time Resident Receives, for as Long as You Need

Respite care at The Pointe at Kirby Gate is a short-term stay — from a few days to several weeks — in a fully furnished suite with the same meals, activities, and 24-hour care our permanent residents enjoy. There is no long-term commitment.

Families use respite care in three common situations:

  • Recovery after a hospital stay or surgery — a safe, supported place to regain strength before returning home
  • A break for family caregivers — travel, rest, or simply time to recharge, knowing your loved one is well cared for
  • A trial stay — experience the community firsthand before making a permanent decision

What’s Included

A Respite Stay Includes

  • Furnished Private SuiteBed, linens, and furnishings ready on arrival — just bring clothes and personal items
  • Three Meals DailyChef-prepared, restaurant-style dining with special diets accommodated
  • 24-Hour Care & AssistanceHelp with bathing, dressing, mobility, and daily needs whenever required
  • Medication ManagementOur nurses handle prescriptions on the same schedule as at home
  • Full Activity CalendarGuests are welcome at every event, meal, and outing
  • Housekeeping & LaundryHandled throughout the stay

How It Works

Arranging a Stay Is Simple

1. Call us. Tell us about your loved one’s needs and the dates you have in mind. We will confirm availability and quote a daily rate.

2. A short assessment. Our nurse reviews care needs and medications so everything is ready on day one.

3. Move in. The suite is furnished and waiting. Most families find that a respite guest settles in by the second day.

Respite care is billed by the day, with no deposit and no long-term contract. Many families who start with a respite stay later choose The Pointe as a permanent home — and the transition is seamless, because the suite, the staff, and the routine are already familiar.

Ask About Availability

Common Questions

Respite Care, Answered

How long can a respite stay last?

Typically from a few days up to about 30 days, depending on availability. Longer stays can be arranged.

Is respite care available for memory care?

Yes. Guests living with Alzheimer’s or dementia stay in our secured memory care neighborhood with specially trained staff.

Does insurance cover respite care?

Respite stays are typically private pay, and some long-term care insurance policies and VA benefits cover respite care. We also accept Medicaid (TennCare) — we are glad to help you check your coverage.

How soon can a stay begin?

Often within a few days. Call (901) 753-3449 to check current availability.
