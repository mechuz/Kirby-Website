Short-Term Stays

Settles In Like Home. Cares Like Family.

Respite care at The Pointe at Kirby Gate is a short-term stay — from a minimum of 5 days up to several weeks — in a cozy, fully furnished suite with warm linens, housekeeping, chef-prepared meals, and the same 24-hour care our permanent residents enjoy. Arrive with a suitcase; everything else is ready.

Families use respite care in three common situations:

  • Recovery after a hospital stay or surgery — a safe, supported place to regain strength before returning home
  • A break for family caregivers — travel, rest, or simply time to recharge, knowing your loved one is well cared for
  • A trial stay — experience the community firsthand before making a permanent decision

Booking rules — please read before you request

  • 5-night minimum — stays run from 5 nights up to about 30 days (longer by arrangement).

  • Rate confirmed before check-in — respite is private pay, billed by the day; we quote your exact daily rate when we confirm.

How It Works

Arranging a Stay Is Simple

1. Request your dates. Use the calendar above (or call us) and tell us about your loved one’s needs. We confirm availability and quote the daily rate within one business day.

2. A short assessment. Our nurse reviews care needs and medications so everything is ready on day one.

3. Check in. The suite is furnished and waiting. Most families find that a respite guest settles in by the second day.

Respite care is private pay, billed by the day, with no deposit and no long-term contract. Many families who start with a respite stay later choose The Pointe as a permanent home — and the transition is seamless, because the suite, the staff, and the routine are already familiar.

Common Questions

Respite Care, Answered

How long can a respite stay last?

Stays start at a 5-day minimum and typically run up to about 30 days, depending on availability. Longer stays can be arranged.

Is respite care available for memory care?

Yes. Guests living with Alzheimer’s or dementia stay in our secured memory care neighborhood with specially trained staff.

How is respite care billed?

Respite stays are private pay, billed by the day, with no deposit and no long-term contract. We confirm your daily rate before check-in — and we are the most affordable community in our class in Memphis.

How soon can a stay begin?

Often within a few days. Request dates above, or call reservations at (901) 247-5835 to check current availability.
