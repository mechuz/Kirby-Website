[P] Compassionate Support in a Secure, Structured Setting
[H2] Specialized Memory Care Services
[P] The Pointe at Kirby Gate provides dedicated memory care for residents living with Alzheimer’s disease or other forms of dementia. Our memory care program focuses on safety, familiarity, and individualized support to help residents feel secure and supported each day.
[P] Care plans are thoughtfully developed to reflect each resident’s cognitive needs, personal history, and daily routines.
[H2] A Secure and Supportive Environment
[P] Memory care at The Pointe at Kirby Gate takes place in a secure area designed to reduce confusion and promote comfort. Clear layouts, consistent routines, and attentive supervision help residents navigate their day with confidence.
[P] This structured environment allows residents to maintain a sense of independence while ensuring their safety and well-being.

[H2] Trained Staff and Meaningful Engagement
[P] Our memory care team receives specialized training to support residents with compassion, patience, and understanding. Staff members focus on creating positive interactions and maintaining dignity through every stage of care.
[P] Activities are designed to encourage cognitive engagement, sensory stimulation, and social connection, helping residents remain engaged and supported throughout the day.
