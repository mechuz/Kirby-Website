About The Pointe at Kirby Gate

A Trusted Assisted Living & Memory Care Community in East Memphis

Our Community

The Pointe at Kirby Gate is an established assisted living and memory care community located in a quiet residential area of East Memphis. Built in 1999 and set on more than 3.5 acres, the community has long served seniors and families seeking dependable care in a calm, supportive environment.

Recently acquired by new ownership, The Pointe at Kirby Gate is entering a new chapter focused on thoughtful improvements to both the physical community and the overall resident experience. These updates reflect a renewed commitment to comfort, safety, and high-quality care while preserving the warm, familiar atmosphere families value.

A Renewed Vision for Senior Living

The vision for The Pointe at Kirby Gate is centered on trust, compassion, and quality of life. Every decision — from renovations to care programs — is guided by the goal of creating a community where residents feel respected, supported, and at home.

By refreshing living spaces, common areas, and the community’s digital presence, The Pointe at Kirby Gate is positioning itself as a modern senior living option that still honors the importance of personal connection, routine, and individualized care.

Assisted Living and Memory Care Under One Roof

The Pointe at Kirby Gate offers both assisted living and dedicated memory care within one thoughtfully designed community. With 60 total beds, including specialized memory care accommodations, residents are able to receive the level of care they need without unnecessary transitions.

This integrated approach allows couples with different care needs to remain close and provides peace of mind for families planning for both current and future care requirements.

Our Commitment to Care and Comfort

Care at The Pointe at Kirby Gate is delivered by a professional, attentive team available 24 hours a day. Personalized support, consistent staffing, and clear communication with families help create a stable and reassuring environment for residents.

Beyond care services, daily life at The Pointe at Kirby Gate includes home-style dining, engaging activities, and shared spaces designed to encourage connection and comfort. The community’s size allows for a more personal experience, where residents are known by name and care is tailored to individual needs.

A Quiet East Memphis Location

The Pointe at Kirby Gate’s location on Quince Road offers residents the benefits of a peaceful residential setting while remaining close to medical offices, shopping, dining, and family access. This balance of convenience and tranquility makes it easier for families to stay connected and for residents to enjoy a comfortable daily routine.

The community’s setting supports a calm pace of life, providing an ideal backdrop for assisted living and memory care services.
