[H2] Welcome to The Pointe at Kirby Gate
[P] Care • Compliance • Community
[P] The Pointe at Kirby Gate is an established assisted living and memory care community located in a quiet residential neighborhood in East Memphis. Set on 3.5 acres, the community offers a supportive, comfortable environment designed to help seniors live with dignity, confidence, and peace of mind.
[P] Under new ownership, The Pointe at Kirby Gate is undergoing thoughtful renovations to refresh both its physical spaces and its overall brand presence. These updates reflect a renewed commitment to quality care, safety, and a welcoming atmosphere for residents and families alike. With 60 total beds, including dedicated memory care accommodations, The Pointe at Kirby Gate provides personalized support in a setting that feels calm, familiar, and secure.

[H2] Assisted Living with Personalized Support
[P] Assisted living at The Pointe at Kirby Gate is designed for seniors who value independence but benefit from daily support. Residents receive personalized assistance with activities of daily living while maintaining control over their routines and lifestyle.
[P] Our professional care team is available 24 hours a day to provide reassurance, support, and attentive service. Residents enjoy private or semi-private accommodations, home-style dining, and access to daily activities that promote social connection, physical movement, and overall well-being.

[H2] Dedicated Memory Care in a Secure Environment
[P] The Pointe at Kirby Gate offers specialized memory care services for residents living with Alzheimer’s disease or other forms of dementia. Our memory care neighborhood provides a secure, structured environment where residents receive compassionate care tailored to their individual needs.
[P] Daily routines, cognitive-focused activities, and consistent staffing help create familiarity and comfort. Families can feel confident knowing their loved ones are supported by trained caregivers in a setting designed to promote safety, engagement, and dignity.
